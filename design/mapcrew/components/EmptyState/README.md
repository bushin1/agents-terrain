# EmptyState

What a screen shows when there is nothing yet: no missions nearby, no payouts, no visits.

- **Provide:** a small map vignette (`MapCrew.map` with a muted pin) in `.mc-empty__art`, a title that states the fact, one sentence on what happens next, and one action.
- Be specific and honest about timing ("Your first $40 lands the Friday after your first visit is validated"). Don't promise volume ("Missions coming soon!").
- No illustrations of people or money.
