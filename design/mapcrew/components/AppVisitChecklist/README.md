# AppVisitChecklist

The on-site flow: seven steps (check in, consent signature, profile setup, manager invite, photos, verification, check out) with a sync indicator in the app bar. "Submit visit" stays disabled, with the number of remaining steps shown under it, until every step is done.

- In the preview, tap steps to complete them and watch Submit unlock.
- The photos step tracks 10 minimum across categories (logo, truck, team, equipment, past jobs) and keeps working offline, with a toast explaining that photos are saved on the phone.
