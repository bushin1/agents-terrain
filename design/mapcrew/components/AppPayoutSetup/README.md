# AppPayoutSetup

Get paid setup: a plain-English summary of the contractor agreement with a typed signature, then a hand-off to Stripe that says exactly what Stripe will ask for and what the company can see, then a confirmation.

- Keep the external-link icon on "Continue to Stripe" so leaving the app is expected.
- Don't use Stripe's logo without their brand approval; the text "Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc." is enough.
